How Much Does Fencing Cost in Melbourne? (2026 Price Guide)

Fencing in Melbourne ranges from $95 per linear metre for timber paling up to $450 per linear metre for frameless glass pool fencing. A standard 18m boundary fence in Colorbond runs around $2,070 supply and install.

These are supply-and-install rates, GST inclusive, for a standard boundary run with reasonable access — not a stripped-down "from" price.

What actually changes the price

  • Material — by far the biggest factor, as the table above shows. Timber and Colorbond dominate standard boundary fencing; glass and steel picket are typically chosen for pools and street-facing frontages.
  • Height — standard boundary height is priced above; pool-compliant or acoustic fencing runs higher.
  • Gates — a single gate or double driveway gate is priced separately, not folded into the per-metre rate.
  • Ground conditions — rocky or uneven ground adds post-hole labour that flat, clear ground doesn't.

Frequently asked questions

How much does fencing cost per metre in Melbourne?

Timber paling fencing costs around $95 per linear metre, Colorbond around $115, aluminium picket around $150, steel picket around $220, and frameless glass pool fencing around $450 per linear metre — all supply and install, GST inclusive.

Is Colorbond or timber fencing cheaper?

Timber paling is typically slightly cheaper upfront than Colorbond, but Colorbond needs no painting or staining and lasts significantly longer, which often makes it the better value choice over time even at a higher initial cost.

Does glass pool fencing need to meet a safety standard?

Yes — frameless glass pool fencing in Victoria must comply with AS1926.1, which governs barrier height, gap sizes, and self-closing gates. This compliance requirement is part of why glass pool fencing costs more than standard boundary fencing.
