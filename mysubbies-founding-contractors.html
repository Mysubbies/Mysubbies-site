Questions contractors ask

Good to know before you apply.

Do I have to accept every job?

No. You decide whether each offered job suits your schedule, area and business.

Is there a joining or subscription fee?

There is no joining fee or subscription for the founding intake. Commercial terms for accepted work are shown during onboarding and before job acceptance.

What do I need to apply?

You will need current business details, ABN, relevant licences or registrations, insurance information, service categories and work areas. Requirements vary by trade.

Can sole traders and companies apply?

Yes. We consider reliable sole traders and established trade businesses, subject to verification and category-specific requirements.

When will I receive jobs?

Offers depend on customer demand, location, approval status and suitability. Mysubbies does not guarantee a minimum volume of work.
