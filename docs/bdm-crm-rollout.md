# BDM CRM rollout

Development branch only. Do not store Google Places preview records in CRM without data-rights review. Use independently verified permitted sources for prospect records. Require authenticated admin actions, approval history, and explicit sending controls. No automated outreach until consent and Australian marketing compliance have been verified.

Stages: (1) isolated database schema; (2) admin-only CRM endpoint; (3) prospect status and draft approval UI; (4) test and review; (5) optional email sending only after separate authorization.
