4. Job Offers and acceptance

4.1 A Job Offer may state the proposed scope, Customer location, attendance window, customer price, Platform Fee, GST treatment, Contractor Payout, required evidence, qualifications and special conditions. It is an invitation to accept, not a promise of allocation.

4.2 A job is allocated only when the portal confirms acceptance. Where multiple eligible contractors receive an offer, the first valid system-confirmed acceptance may receive the job. A notification delay, cached screen or technical error does not create an allocation.

4.3 Before accepting, the Contractor must assess the available information and its capacity to perform. Acceptance commits the Contractor to the scope, time and payout displayed, subject to an approved Variation.

4.4 The Contractor may decline a Job Offer without penalty. After acceptance, cancellation is permitted for genuine safety, legal, health, emergency or materially inaccurate-scope reasons, but the Contractor must notify MySubbies promptly and provide reasonable supporting information.

  • promptly investigate and rectify defects, omissions or damage attributable to its work, without limiting any statutory remedy.

The Contractor must not engage in harassment, discrimination, violence, threatening conduct, intoxication at work, dishonesty, unsafe conduct, false reviews or misleading claims.
