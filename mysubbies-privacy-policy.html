Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026 · Last updated: 5 September 2026

1. About this policy

Mysubbies Holdings Pty Ltd (ABN 69 693 675 268) (MySubbies, we, us or our) operates the MySubbies websites, applications, customer portal, contractor portal and related services (Platform). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information and how you may access, correct or complain about our handling of it.

We intend to handle personal information consistently with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) and other applicable Australian laws. Where the APPs do not legally apply to a particular activity, we will use this policy as our operational standard to the extent reasonably practicable.

2. Personal information we collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect:

  • identity and contact details, such as name, date of birth where required, address, email and telephone number;
  • account details, login credentials, preferences, communications and support history;
  • booking and property details, job descriptions, measurements, access instructions, preferred times, quotes, variations, invoices, warranties and dispute records;
  • photos, video, audio or documents uploaded to describe a job, which may incidentally show people, vehicle registrations, home interiors, security systems or other private information;
  • transaction information, payment status, refunds and limited payment identifiers (full card or bank details are generally handled by our payment provider);
  • contractor information, including business details, ABN, trade categories, qualifications, registrations, licences, insurance, work history, availability, location, identity-check results and bank payout details;
  • device and usage information, such as IP address, device identifiers, browser type, app activity, pages viewed, approximate location, cookie identifiers and diagnostic logs;
  • reviews, ratings, survey responses, referrals and content submitted to the Platform;
  • fraud, safety, compliance and incident information; and
  • any other information you provide or authorise us to collect.

Sensitive information. We do not generally seek sensitive information. If it is reasonably necessary for a specific function, we will collect it with consent or as otherwise permitted by law. Please avoid uploading unnecessary health information, identity documents or images of people not involved in a booking.

3. How we collect information

We may collect information directly from you, automatically through the Platform, from the other party to a booking, from referees or authorised representatives, from identity, licence, insurance or background-check providers, from payment and communications providers, from public registers and publicly available sources, and where authorised or required by law.

If you give us personal information about another person, you must be authorised to do so and, where practicable, make them aware of this policy. You may use a pseudonym or interact anonymously where lawful and practicable, but identification is normally required to create an account, book or perform work, receive payment, manage safety or comply with law.

4. Why we use personal information

We may use personal information to:

  • create, verify, administer and secure accounts;
  • generate indicative prices or job scopes, match users, arrange bookings and enable communications;
  • share job details with prospective or selected contractors and share contractor details with customers;
  • process, hold, release or refund payments and recover amounts due;
  • provide customer support and manage cancellations, incidents, insurance matters, complaints and disputes;
  • check identity, licences, registrations, insurance and eligibility, and monitor quality and safety;
  • detect, investigate and prevent fraud, misuse, prohibited conduct and security incidents;
  • operate, troubleshoot, analyse and improve the Platform, including automated tools used to classify jobs or produce indicative prices;
  • send service messages and, with any consent required by law, marketing communications;
  • enforce agreements, establish or defend legal claims and comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting and reporting obligations; and
  • conduct business administration, audits, corporate transactions and professional advice.

Automated tools may help suggest categories, measurements, prices, risk flags or contractor matches. Unless the Platform clearly states otherwise, these outputs are estimates or recommendations and may be reviewed or changed after further information or a site inspection.

5. Disclosure of personal information

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to:

  • customers, contractors and authorised representatives involved in a requested or confirmed booking;
  • payment processors, banks, identity and licence-verification providers, insurers, communications providers, cloud hosting, analytics, mapping, customer-support, document and security providers;
  • professional advisers, auditors, debt recovery providers and dispute-resolution bodies;
  • government agencies, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, courts or other persons where authorised or required by law;
  • a purchaser, investor or adviser in connection with a proposed or completed business transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections; and
  • other persons with your consent or at your direction.

We do not sell personal information. A contractor who receives customer information must use it only for the relevant booking, safety, record-keeping and legal purposes and must protect it from unauthorised use or disclosure.

6. Overseas disclosures

Some service providers may store or process information outside Australia. The likely countries may include, e.g. United States, Singapore, Ireland and other locations used by the provider. Where required, we will take reasonable steps to ensure an overseas recipient handles personal information consistently with the APPs, subject to any legal exceptions. Provider locations may change; current information may be requested from our Privacy Officer.

7. Cookies, analytics and communications

We may use cookies, pixels, software development kits and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure performance, improve the Platform, prevent fraud and understand campaign effectiveness. Browser or device settings may block some technologies, but parts of the Platform may then not work properly.

We may send operational messages needed to manage an account or booking. Marketing messages will include an unsubscribe method where required. You may opt out of marketing at any time, but cannot opt out of essential service, safety, security or legal notices while using the Platform.

8. Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You must keep credentials confidential and notify us promptly of suspected unauthorised access.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including booking administration, warranties, disputes, fraud prevention, taxation, accounting, insurance and legal compliance. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to destroy it or de-identify it, unless retention is required or authorised by law or technically necessary in secure backups for a limited period.

If an eligible data breach occurs, we will investigate, contain and notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

9. Access and correction

You may request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you by contacting the Privacy Officer. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within a reasonable period and may refuse access or correction where permitted by law, giving reasons and available complaint options where required. We generally do not charge for making a request, although a permitted reasonable access cost may apply after notice.

10. Privacy complaints

Send a privacy complaint to the Privacy Officer using the details below, describing the issue and desired outcome. We will acknowledge and investigate the complaint and aim to provide a response within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au.

11. Children

The Platform is intended for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly permit children to create customer or contractor accounts. If we learn that we collected a child's personal information without appropriate authority, we will take reasonable steps to delete or otherwise lawfully manage it.

12. Third-party links and changes

The Platform may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Platform, providers or law. We will publish the updated policy and, where a change is material, provide reasonable notice through the Platform or by email.
